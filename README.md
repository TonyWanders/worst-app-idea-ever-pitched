## The pitch

> PuddleWatch is the AI app that photographs every puddle you step around, rates its splash potential, and emails your friends a weekly report so they know exactly which puddles you heroically avoided, for only five dollars a month.

One sentence, 230 characters. A product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody on earth needs.

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic. It writes the pitch above to `/output/entry.txt` (or `./output/entry.txt` when `/output` is not writable) and does nothing else. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
